## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm, clean, minimal dark portfolio for a fullstack engineer — calm and premium, not technical-costume; it refuses both the old instrument chrome and the generic neon/glass dark mode.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-black field (#141310, #0f0e0c deep) with subtle raised surfaces (#1b1a16). Warm off-white ink (#ece8e1) with muted (#9a948a) and faint (#8a857b) warm grays. One understated amber accent (#d9a462) used sparingly. Refined serif display (Newsreader) for the name and headings; clean geometric sans (Manrope) for body and UI. Hairline rules (9% white) and soft 1rem radius; subtle, lift-on-hover project surfaces with a blurred sticky header.

STORY: A visitor understands Leonardo is a thoughtful full-stack engineer in one clean view, reads the three real projects, sees the fullstack disciplines, and emails him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header (name + anchored nav). Hero: small amber "Fullstack Engineer" eyebrow, a large serif statement ("I build digital products end to end."), a muted one-line intro, and the primary Email-me pill plus a LinkedIn link. All within the first viewport at desktop and mobile.

FORM: Warm dark minimal; signature mechanics are the subtle lift-on-hover project cards keyed by an amber index number, the blurred sticky header, and the single amber accent that marks links, focus, and the eyebrow.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
