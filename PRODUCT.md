# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are hiring managers, technical interviewers, and recruiting engineers who are evaluating the site owner as a software engineer candidate. Their job: quickly judge skill level, breadth, and fit, review the work, and decide whether to open a conversation or extend an offer.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents the owner as a software engineer — who they are, what they can build, and the work that demonstrates it — so a stranger can assess them as a candidate and get in touch. Success means a visitor who arrived cold ends up convinced and contacts the owner.

## Positioning

The owner is a full-stack generalist: comfortable working across the whole stack rather than confined to one layer. The portfolio should let that breadth read instantly while still showing depth where it matters.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a resume, LinkedIn, GitHub, or a referral and are often skimming several candidates. Assessment is quick and comparative, so the page must communicate capability fast, expose real work, and make contact frictionless.

## Product Principles

1. Prove capability with real work, never invented claims.
2. Sell breadth (full stack) and earn trust through specificity.
3. Make assessment fast and skimmable for a busy technical reader.
4. Keep contact obvious and low-friction.
5. Let the owner's identity be genuine and personal, not a stock persona.
